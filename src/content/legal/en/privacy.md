This policy explains how {{legalName}} ("01 Capital", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data through this website.

## What we collect

- **Deck submissions.** When you submit the form, we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, company, website, country, what you're looking for, your pillar and stage, the amount you're raising, a description of your company, a link to your deck, and how you heard about us.
- **Messages.** If you email us, we keep your message and contact details.
- **Technical data.** Our hosting and security providers process technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to deliver the website, prevent abuse and measure overall traffic. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How we use it

We use your details to review your submission, contact you about it, keep a record of the companies we've met, and protect the website from spam and abuse.

## Why we're allowed to use it

We use submission data because you've asked us to review your company, and with the consent you give on the form. We use technical data because we have a legitimate interest in running a secure website.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell personal data. We use service providers that process data on our behalf:

- **Brevo**, to store submissions and send emails.
- **Cloudflare**, to host and secure the website, check that forms are filled in by people rather than bots, and measure traffic without cookies.

These providers may process data outside your country. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

## How long we keep it

We keep submission data while we evaluate and follow up on your submission, and then for up to two years so we can recognize returning founders. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw your consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, email us at the address below.

## Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to the people who need it. No way of sending data over the internet is completely secure.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
